**The Octopus Ate the Wedding** — a colossal octopus in a tiny chef's hat bursts
through the ceiling of a fancy restaurant kitchen, tentacles snatching plates
out of the air while the chef screams and throws a pan and a waiter slips on
spilled soup, sauce flying everywhere in warm dramatic light. One giant
octopus, one frozen moment of chaos.

The entry program validates the image (PNG/JPEG magic bytes, at most 1 MiB,
at most 1024×1024 px) and copies it to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
