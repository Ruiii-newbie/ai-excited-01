through the ceiling of a fine restaurant kitchen, tentacles snatching pasta
plates out of the air. The chef screams and hurls a frying pan, a waiter
slides across the soup-slicked floor, and red sauce arcs through warm golden
light. One huge octopus, one impossible frozen moment.

The program checks the entry image first — PNG/JPEG signature, no more than
1 MiB, no more than 1024×1024 px — and then copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
